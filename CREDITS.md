- **Furniture Kit, City Kit, Suburban Houses Pack, Survival Kit** (`Assets/*-glb` folders): these folders ship without a licence file. Their names and style match Kenney's CC0 packs, but the author has not been confirmed. Verify before any public release.
- **game-icons.net** icons (`Assets/game-icons.net.png`): CC BY 3.0. Authors used include Lorc and Andy Meneely. See https://game-icons.net for each icon's author.

- **Dish and ingredient illustrations** (`Assets/dishes`, `Assets/sprites`): generated with the jam-provided image model, then cleaned up by hand.
